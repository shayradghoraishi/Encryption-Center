# Code of Conduct

We want Encryption Center to be a technical project where people can discuss security, implementation and design without harassment.

Expected behavior:

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Explain technical disagreements with evidence where possible.
- Do not publish another person's private information.
- Do not intentionally disrupt the project or its infrastructure.

Project maintainers may remove content or restrict participation when necessary to keep the project usable and safe.
